Why SeniorSecured.org Exists

Fred Flamer· Founder, SeniorSecured.org

Over the years, working in law enforcement, I have witnessed the aftermath of many different types of crime. But few have troubled me more than crimes involving the manipulation, deception, and financial exploitation of our senior population.

What bothers me most is the realization that many of these crimes might have been prevented with something surprisingly simple: basic knowledge, awareness, and a little practical know-how.

Let’s face it. Our seniors have gotten the short end of the stick when it comes to education and training designed specifically to help them navigate today’s rapidly evolving technological landscape.

Technology has advanced at a breathtaking pace, but the opportunities for many older adults to understand its risks have not always kept up.

Unfortunately, criminals recognize this information gap and are more than willing to exploit it.

That is why SeniorSecured.org exists.

My mission is straightforward: to help bridge that knowledge gap through education, awareness, practical training, and information sharing. Not to frighten seniors about technology, but to empower them to use it with greater confidence, independence, and security.

Cybercriminals are constantly adapting their methods, developing new schemes, and finding creative ways to exploit unsuspecting individuals.

I believe education is one of our most effective defenses.

Knowledge really is power. And knowledge shared and taught is knowledge multiplied.

If SeniorSecured.org can help even one person recognize a scam, avoid financial exploitation, or protect their independence, then this effort has already made a meaningful difference.

Because protecting our seniors isn’t simply about preventing crime.

It’s about protecting their dignity, confidence, independence, and peace of mind.
— Fred Flamer
Founder, SeniorSecured.org
